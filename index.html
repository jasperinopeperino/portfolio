Hi there! I’m Jasper, a graphic designer and type geek based in The Hague, the Netherlands. I’m interested in the everyday and the spaces we move through, especially the details that often go unnoticed. My work moves across graphic design, typography and physical interventions. I like to observe how people interact with their surroundings and use design to question familiar environments, shift perspectives, and find new ways of looking at the world around us.

When I’m not designing, you can find me between the goalposts for my local football team or out on my bike. If you’d like to chat about why we never see a pigeon sitting or why I’m part of Tweeb, please get in touch!
